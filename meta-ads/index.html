More than campaign setup

I run the ads, read the data, and automate the reporting.

Most ad managers stop at launching campaigns. I also build the data pipeline and dashboards behind them, so every budget decision is backed by up-to-date numbers.

Set up & optimise

Full-funnel structure in Ads Manager — awareness, traffic and sales — with naming conventions, audiences, budgets and creative testing.

Analyse

Looker Studio dashboards per objective, filterable by ad set and date, so every creative is judged on the metric that matters for its stage.

Automate

Google Apps Script pulls ad-level Meta data into Google Sheets every day. No manual exports, and the dashboards are always up to date.

Insight from the data

One creative brought in half the traffic at less than half the cost.

The traffic dashboard showed that Morning Chill Club delivered 5,621 landing page views at Rp144 each, while the other creatives cost Rp327–534.

Six days in, the two weakest ads were paused so the budget could go to the creatives that were working.

Framework

A structured approach to profitable scaling.

Funnel-first structure

Separate campaigns for awareness, traffic and sales, each optimised for its own result.

Creative iteration

Several hooks and formats per product. Winners get budget, weak ads get paused early.

Automated reporting

Daily data in Sheets and Looker Studio, so decisions are based on numbers, not guesses.

Budget scaling

Scale what is proven, retarget warm visitors, and protect ROAS as spend grows.

The optimisation loop

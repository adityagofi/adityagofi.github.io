The Ads Framework

Funnel-first structure

Separate campaigns for awareness, traffic and sales, each optimised for its own result.

Creative iteration

Several hooks and formats per product. Winners get budget, weak ads get paused early.

Automated reporting

Daily data in Sheets and Looker Studio, so decisions are based on numbers.

Budget scaling

Scale what is proven, retarget warm visitors, and protect ROAS as spend grows.

Every week

The optimisation loop

  1. 1Read the dashboards by objective and creative
  2. 2Pause ads above target cost per result
  3. 3Move budget to the winning creative
  4. 4Brief new hooks based on what worked
  5. 5Share a clear summary with the client

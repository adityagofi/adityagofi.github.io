Marketing analytics · Page by Page

From ad click to website behaviour, in one view.

For a local perfume brand, I connected Meta Ads data, Google Analytics 4 and Looker Studio to answer one question: which ads bring people who actually engage and buy? These are the dashboards and the findings from one month of data.

Campaign impact

One product page took 59% of all views

The Nonchalant product page drew 14.2k of 23.9k views, peaking from 5 to 11 October while the Nonchalant sales ads were running. Paid social brought 6k sessions, the second-largest source after direct.

Recommendation

Clicks arrive, but they don't stay

Average engagement on the Nonchalant product page was about 1 second, compared with 22 seconds on the homepage and 1m 49s on the Meet Your Alter Ego page. The next test: send part of the traffic to richer pages and compare checkouts.

Search demand

Ads are building brand search

Brand queries such as “page by page” make up most of the 259 organic clicks, at an average position of 3.5. Product names like “morning chill club” are now searched and rank in the top three.
